Current Research

Job Market Paper

Extending Postpartum Medicaid Without Discouraging Work: Evidence from the 12-Month Coverage Extension

Using American Community Survey data and a triple-differences design, I study the twelve-month postpartum Medicaid extension and its effects on insurance coverage and maternal labor supply.

The extension increased Medicaid coverage among low-income recent mothers by roughly 3–4 percentage points, with no systematic reduction in employment, labor-force participation, or hours worked.
