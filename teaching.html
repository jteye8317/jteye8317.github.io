Teaching

I teach economics the way I do research: by starting from a real question, reasoning carefully from evidence, and helping students become confident users of quantitative evidence.

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching emphasizes clear reasoning, active problem-solving, empirical applications, and structured practice. I want students to leave able to evaluate quantitative claims, work confidently with data, and use economics as a tool for answering real questions.

I use worked examples, active participation, data exercises, and applied projects to connect statistical and economic concepts to problems students can recognize and investigate.
