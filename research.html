Job Market Paper · 2026

Extending Postpartum Medicaid Without Discouraging Work

Evidence from the 12-Month Coverage Extension

The American Rescue Plan Act allowed states to extend pregnancy-related Medicaid coverage from sixty days to twelve months postpartum. Using American Community Survey data and a triple-differences design, I compare recent mothers with mothers of older children in states where the extension was legally in force versus states where it was not when Medicaid redeterminations resumed in 2023.

Under the preferred legal-effective-date coding, the extension increased Medicaid coverage among low-income recent mothers by roughly 3–4 percentage points, with no systematic reduction in employment, labor-force participation, or hours worked.
